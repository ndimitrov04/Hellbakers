## Introduction

Welcome to the oven... and prepare to get cooked!

Collect monster cards by playing a slot machine. The more you play, the higher the chance you get cursed cards which will harm your progress.

The most important rule in this kitchen is:

> ### ⚠️ DON'T GET FRIED

## Gameplay

### 🎰 Feeling Lucky?

Use **soul dough** as currency to play the slot machine for powerful kitchen monster cards and destroy your enemies. Be careful though — the less soul dough you have when pulling the lever, the higher the chance of getting a **cursed monster card** instead! Don't burn your meal.

![Slot Machine](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMzg0ODczNi8yMjk1NDY3NS5wbmc=/original/oe18gK.png)

### 👹 Cursed Monsters

Cursed monsters must be placed before the battle starts. They will fight for the **enemy team** against you! Now that's some pretty raw competition.

![Cursed Monster](https://img.itch.zone/aW1nLzIyOTU0ODgxLnBuZw==/original/pV9PCN.png)

### 🍪 Risk it for a Biscuit? Why Stop at One?

For each stage cleared you earn one **biscuit**. Biscuits are spent at the after-death shop for:

- Powerful permanent upgrades to all possible cards
- Improved slot machine odds for non-cursed monsters

![Biscuit Shop](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMzg0ODczNi8yMjk1NDY3Ni5wbmc=/794x1000/wNEF01.png)

### 🐾 Monster Types

Different monsters have different behaviors. Learn all of them to master the game! Numbers aren't everything — maybe with some wit and luck you could bake a cake out of it.
